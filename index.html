Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Notion mix up my columns when I import a CSV?

If a field contains a comma (like an address or a list of tags) and isn't strictly enclosed in RFC-4180 quotes, Notion interprets the comma as a new column separator, shifting every subsequent column to the right.

Why are my dates imported as plain text instead of Notion Date properties?

Notion's date parser reliably recognizes ISO 8601 (YYYY-MM-DD). Formats like 03/12/2026 often cause month/day confusion or get categorized as regular text fields.

What happens to non-UTF-8 and control characters?

Hidden control characters (such as null bytes, bell codes, and byte-order markers) are safely stripped away to prevent import errors in Notion.

Can I run this offline?

Yes. All logic is self-contained and functions fully without internet connectivity.
